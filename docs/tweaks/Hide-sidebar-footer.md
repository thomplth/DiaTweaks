# Hide sidebar footer

With tabs in the sidebar, Dia keeps a bar along its bottom: the dots that switch spaces, the recent tabs button and the
others beside them. This tweak hides the whole bar, and the tab list grows down into its room.

Spaces still switch with a two-finger swipe over the sidebar or with their keyboard shortcuts.

It changes nothing with tabs at the top.

Turn it on in **Settings → Patches**, or with `diatweaks enable hideSidebarFooter`.
